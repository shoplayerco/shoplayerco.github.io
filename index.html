About Layer & Co.

A local print studio with a personal touch.

Layer & Co. is a San Diego-based 3D-printing studio creating character-inspired models, gifts, collectibles, and custom pieces. We focus on clean prints, dependable quality, and friendly service.

Whether you already have a design in mind or just an idea, we’ll help turn it into something you can hold.
